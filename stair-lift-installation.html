How Buying Through Us Works

You get manufacturer-direct equipment with local, certified installation — the best of both worlds.

1

Talk With Us

Call or send the form. We help you pick the right lift or scooter for your home and budget.

2

On-Site Assessment

For stair lifts and VPLs we visit, measure, and photograph the site — the measurements feed CAD drawings and engineering for an exact quote. A site assessment fee applies.

3

Dealer Pricing Quote

You get a written quote with your dealer-direct equipment price plus installation — pricing varies by model, stairway shape, and site conditions.

4

Certified Installation

Justin personally installs and tests your equipment, walks you through every feature, and registers your manufacturer warranty.

Why Buy Through a Certified Dealer?

AmeriGlide sells many lifts online for self-installation — but a lift is only as safe as its install.

Dealer-Direct Pricing

We order at dealer cost and pass the savings on — your equipment quote is typically below what you'd pay buying retail online, installed.

Measured Right the First Time

Curved lifts and VPLs need CAD-accurate measurements. Our on-site assessment catches the problems self-installers discover too late.

Local Service After the Sale

Warranty claims run through us, not a 1-800 call queue. If your lift needs service, you call Justin — he installed it.
