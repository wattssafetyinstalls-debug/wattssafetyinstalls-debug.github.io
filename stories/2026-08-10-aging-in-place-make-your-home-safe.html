Aging in Place: Make Your Home Safe

By Justin Watts · Watts ATP Contractor

Stay Safe, Stay Home!

Want to age comfortably in your own home? Let's make it safe! Simple changes can prevent accidents and boost independence for years.

Bathroom Bliss, Not Busts!

Slippery surfaces are risky. Install grab bars near toilets and in showers. Non-slip mats are a must for peace of mind in your bathroom.

Brighten Your Path!

Good lighting prevents trips and falls. Add nightlights, especially in hallways. For folks in Norfolk, NE, clear pathways are absolutely key!

Clear Clutter, Clear Mind!

Remove tripping hazards like loose rugs and cords. Keep frequently used items within easy reach. Simplify your space for safer, stress-free living.
