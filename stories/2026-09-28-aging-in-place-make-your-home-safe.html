By Justin Watts · Watts ATP Contractor

Stay Home, Stay Safe!

Want to age comfortably in your own home? Let's make it a safe haven, right here in Norfolk, NE!

Clear Paths, Prevent Falls

Remove clutter, loose rugs, and tangled cords. Keep walkways clear for easy movement throughout your home.

Bathroom Safety First!

Install grab bars in showers and next to toilets. Non-slip mats are also crucial. Small changes make a big difference.

Light Up Your Life!

Ensure good lighting in all areas, especially stairs and hallways. Motion-sensor lights are a smart, easy upgrade.
