Fall Prevention Tips for Seniors

By Justin Watts · Watts ATP Contractor

Stay Steady, Stay Safe!

Falls are a top concern for seniors. Let's look at simple ways to make your home safer and keep you on your feet.

Clear Clutter, Prevent Trips

Remove rugs, cords, and general clutter from walkways. A clear path is a safe path, especially here in Norfolk, NE!

Brighten Up Your Home

Ensure good lighting in all areas, especially stairs and hallways. Nightlights are your friends for those late-night trips.

Strong Legs, Stable Steps

Regular, gentle exercise improves balance and strength. Consult your doctor about appropriate activities to stay active.
